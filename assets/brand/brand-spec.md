# CueX brand master

The logo is extracted from the closing mark of the original CueX Remotion film, following the user's request in this session. The two crossing strokes retain their amber `#FF7A2F` and lavender `#8E8CFF` colors. The first stroke denotes the fast decision layer; the second denotes slower preparation. Their intersection is the output decision.

- `cuex-symbol.svg`: primary two-stroke X, used by the web navigation and favicon.
- `cuex-symbol-mono.svg`: single-ink X for monochrome output.
- `cuex-wordmark.svg`: Instrument Serif “Cue” converted to vector outlines, followed by the X, for light surfaces.
- `cuex-wordmark-light.svg`: the same outlined wordmark for dark surfaces.

Outlined wordmarks require no installed fonts. The film retains its existing animated CueXMark component for the stroke reveal. Do not add unrelated icon geometry. The main product retains its grey-green/cinnabar clinical palette; the two logo colors are a brand-specific exception, not a new UI palette.

Fonts: Instrument Serif from the project's existing Google Fonts / OFL asset. Chinese product UI: Noto Sans SC. Doctor voice: Noto Serif SC. Source font provenance is retained in video/src/assets/manifest.json.
