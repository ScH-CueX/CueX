# Six pitch directions

Shared brief: spec.md. All designs are 2050 × 574, the exact 10.25 : 2.87 ratio. Existing CueX branding is the constraint; the X is retained in every version.

1. 诊室里的停顿. Designer lens: Naoto Fukasawa's attention to unconscious, readable behavior. Ground is the actual grey-green clinic, black/serif voice contrast, real plaster doctor crop on the right, spare copy on the left. Bold choice: give the silent doctor most of the height. Form comes from the nonverbal turn-taking signal. SELECT for full cover and interaction page.
2. 两道思考的交点. Designer lens: Studio Dumbar's identity-as-motion approach, applied as two precise static trajectories. Original dark ink, amber/lavender X, enormous wordmark offset left, actual product strip on the opposite half. Bold choice: make the crossing strokes the composition. Form comes from fast decision and slower preparation meeting at output. SELECT.
3. 单一产品主角. Reality reference: Apple WWDC24 keynote, verified official video https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/101/ . One large product image, one sentence, generous margins. Reference capture attempted separately; do not claim a specific frame was viewed without its screenshot. Backup.
4. 并行的两条轨道. Reality reference: Stripe Sessions 2025, verified official archive https://stripe.com/sessions/2025 . Broad horizontal flow with a larger right-hand product demonstration. Capture attempted separately; backup.
5. 一句话，接住变化. Roulette: observed seconds S=55; PPT selection 55%20+1=16, Institutional Swiss Minimal. Adapt to the existing CueX colors. Three concise behavior statements run across the ultra-wide canvas, one real UI crop is the proof. Bold choice: strong typographic tension and one cinnabar field, no claims of numerical outcomes. SELECT.
6. 情境色谱. East roulette 55%12+1=8, Sugiura Information Mandala; this radial information structure fights the extremely wide screen and risks clutter. Keep a simplified concentric concern diagram only as a backup thumbnail; not selected.

The selected three deliberately have different skeletons: image-led asymmetry, dark crossing-stroke identity, and wide typographic evidence. No production deck is selected yet.
